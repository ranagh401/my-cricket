How to play
  • Teams: both players pick an XI of real cricketers within a budget of 300 credits. Better players cost more. You need enough bowlers to cover the overs. Batting order is the order of your list.
  • Toss: the joining player calls. The winner bats or bowls first.
  • Bowling: pick a bowler for each over (no bowler bowls two in a row). Choose line, length and pace, press Bowl, then Release when the marker is in the green. Spinners turn the ball after it pitches.
  • Batting: choose Defend, Attack or Loft, then press Leg, Straight or Off as the ball reaches the bat. Good players have a more forgiving timing window.
  • Umpire and DRS: close lbw and caught-behind calls go to the umpire, who can get them wrong. The side that disagrees can review within 12 seconds. Ball tracking or UltraEdge decides. A failed review is lost; umpire's call keeps it.
  • Keyboard: bowling uses ← ↑ → for line, A S D for length, F to switch pace and Space to bowl and release. Batting uses 1 2 3 for the shot type and ← ↑ → to play. R reviews, Enter accepts.
  • Leaving mid-match forfeits it. A player who drops off the wifi has one minute to come back.
